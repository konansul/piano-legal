Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Piano ("the app", "we", "us"), published on the App Store as Piano: Learn Songs, teaches you to play songs on an on-screen keyboard. This policy explains what happens to your data. In short: there is no account, your progress and your name stay on your iPhone, and what the app does send is anonymous: how the app is used, reports of crashes, and the state of your subscription. If you have questions, contact us at konansulx@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

We have no server of our own. What the app sends goes to the services below, and none of it names you.

  • No account, no sign-in. There is nothing to register. The app never asks for your email address, phone number or password.
  • Anonymous usage. The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to record what happens in it: which screens are opened, which song was started or finished, with which hands, at what speed and how accurately (in broad bands, such as 80 to 89%), the answers to the first-launch questions (why you play and how much you have played), the daily goal and reminder hour you chose, how a paywall was opened and closed, and whether a purchase went through, with the product and its price. With these, Firebase receives the device model, the iOS version, the app version, an approximate location worked out from the IP address (country and region, never a precise position) and a random identifier for this installation. Your name is never sent. Google keeps this data for 14 months. You can turn it off at any time in the app under Settings, Share anonymous usage. You can read Google's terms at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Crash reports. If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a report: where in the app it happened, the device model and the iOS version. Reports contain nothing you typed and are kept for 90 days.
  • Subscriptions. The app uses Superwall to decide which paywall and which offer to show and to know whether Premium is active. Superwall receives a random identifier for this installation, the device's vendor identifier, the app and iOS versions, which paywall was shown, and, from Apple, the state of the subscription: trial started, renewed, cancelled or refunded. You can read Superwall's policy at superwall.com/privacy.
  • No tracking, no advertising. The app does not request the advertising identifier, does not track you across other apps or websites, shows no ads, and sells or shares nothing for advertising.
  • Works without a network. Every song, picture and sound is inside the app. Playing, practising and your progress all work offline. The network is used only for what is listed above and for the App Store.

Google and Superwall process this data on our behalf, on servers that may be outside your country, including in the United States.

2. Data that stays on your device

  • Your name. If you tell the app what to call you, the name is stored only on your iPhone and used to greet you and in your reminders. It is never sent anywhere. You can change or clear it in Settings.
  • Your progress. Stars, best accuracy, the songs you have played and the minutes you practised each day are kept in the app's own storage on the device. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Reminders. If you allow notifications, the app schedules its daily reminders on your iPhone itself. Their text, which may include the song to continue and your name, is written on the device and never passes through a server. You can turn reminders off in the app or in iOS Settings.
  • No microphone, camera, photos or contacts. The app asks for none of them. You play on the screen.

3. Purchases

Piano is free to install, and the first level is free to play. Piano Premium opens every song and level, both hands together, slower speeds and looping. It is an auto-renewing subscription; the plans and any free trial on offer are shown in the app before you subscribe, and they can differ from person to person while we test which works best. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, and we never see your name or payment details. The app asks Apple whether a subscription is active, and Apple also tells Superwall, as described above.

4. Children

The app is rated 4+ and can be used by a child with a parent. It is not directed at children, asks no one for contact details, and the name field is optional. It collects the same anonymous data described above from everyone who uses it, and anonymous usage can be turned off in Settings.

5. Your choices

You can turn off anonymous usage in Settings, and deleting the app stops everything above. Because nothing we receive is linked to who you are, we usually cannot pick out one person's data, but if you would like us to try, to tell you what we hold or to delete it, write to us and we will do what Google and Superwall allow.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
