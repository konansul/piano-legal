Piano

Learn piano with real songs

Piano teaches you to play with notes that fall onto the keys: the song waits for your finger, lights every key you press and gives you up to five stars for each run. There are 33 songs in five levels, from Twinkle, Twinkle and Jingle Bells to Für Elise, Clair de Lune and The Entertainer, all in the public domain and arranged for the app. You can play one hand or both, slow a song down and loop the hard part, or just play freely on the keys.

There is no account. Your progress, your name if you give one and your settings stay on your iPhone. The app counts anonymous usage, which you can turn off in Settings, and sends anonymous crash reports.
